Privacy Policy for Beret Buddies

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Beret Buddies ("the app") is a free French-learning app for children and families. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Who we are

Beret Buddies is made by Tran Dang Vu, an individual developer. Contact: trandangvu@gmail.com

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request the INTERNET permission.
  • It does not collect or send your email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. The only personal detail the app can hold is the optional first name described below, and it never leaves the device.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not upload or share anything with us or with anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Learning progress (lessons finished, stars, days practised, review schedule, settings) and the first name the learner may type on the welcome screen so the app can greet them. The name is optional, can be changed or removed in Settings, and is used only to show greetings inside the app. It is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. You can export it as a file from Settings and import it again.
  • Backup by Android. If Google Backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may copy this progress to your own Google account, so that it can come back after a reinstall. This is done by Android under your own Google account settings. We cannot see it, and you can turn it off in your phone settings.

Progress report

The "My progress" page can show a short text summary (days practised, lessons finished, what the learner can do). It is copied to the clipboard only when the learner taps "Copy my report"; the app itself never sends it anywhere.

Microphone

The "Say it" button listens through the microphone only after you tap it, so you can practise speaking French. The speech is checked on the device by a recogniser that is built into the app. The audio is kept in memory while it is checked, then discarded. It is never saved, never sent anywhere. The app works without the microphone: if you refuse the permission, everything except "Say it" still works.

Spoken French

The app reads French aloud with voices that are built into the app and run on the device. The voices are AI-generated. Sentences that have been read once are kept as small audio files in the app's private storage, so they play faster next time; these files are not included in Android backups and are deleted when you uninstall the app. If you switch the built-in voices off in Settings, the app uses the text-to-speech voices already installed on your phone instead (for example Google's French voice). That is handled by your phone's own speech service, under that service's own privacy policy. In both cases the app does not send text anywhere.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It collects no personal data, shows no ads, has no chat or social features, and has no links that take a child outside the app. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for a parent to delete on our side.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all progress stored on the device. Backups in your own Google account can be managed in your phone's Google settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
